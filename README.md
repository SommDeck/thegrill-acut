### SommDeck | Exclusive Demo for The Grill by A cut

Welcome to the bespoke demonstration of **SommDeck**, a digital wine list and stock management system tailored specifically for the premium beverage program at **The Grill by A cut**. 

Co-developed by a senior sommelier and an otaku developer, this system delivers meticulous database precision disguised as an elegant, high-performance guest interface. 

### Dedicated Features for This Demo:

*   **A Cut Curated Layout**: Designed to mirror the sophistication of your dining room, powered seamlessly by a connected Google Sheet. 
*   **Sommelier-Centric Filtering**: Instant sorting by specific **grape varieties** and **custom tags** (e.g., bin numbers, allocation status, or pairing recommendations). 
*   **Two-Way Real-Time Inventory**: Experience how floor sales instantly sync with back-of-house stock logs, preventing the awkward "86" on premium vintages mid-service.
